# Coupled stripline on a board

Two traces on the inner layer of a three-layer board, its outer layers the region's conducting lid
and floor, meshed by the planar mesher with ports on the region's sides. The port's two
quasi-TEM modes — equal currents, then opposite ones — are compared with Cohn's exact even and
odd impedances of zero-thickness coupled strips (the same geometry as `coupled-stripline-impedance`
on the structured grid).
