# Bragg stop band of an axially rippled circular guide

One period of a circular guide whose wall waves about its axis, r = r₀ + h sin(2πz/p) (the
pulse compressor template's cell, a SolidCraft design revolved, here with h = 1 mm), solved for
its TE11 Bloch wave on the half-plane (`solve.revolution.bloch`). The Bragg frequency, where the
wave's phase per period is π, lies in the stop band the ripple opens; below it the guide passes.
