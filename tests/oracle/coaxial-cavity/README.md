# Coaxial cavity

A coaxial line (conductors of radius 1 and 3.5 mm, air between) shorted by conducting plates at
both ends, 30 mm long: the `coaxial-cavity` template without its copper walls. Its TEM
resonances sit where the length holds p half wavelengths, f = p c/(2l), independent of the
cross-section: a check of the inner conductor as a body of metal in the eigenmode solver.
