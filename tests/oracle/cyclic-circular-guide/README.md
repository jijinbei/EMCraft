# Circular guide on a quarter

The straight circular guide of `circular-guide` (radius 10 mm, 30 mm long, ports on both ends)
meshed on one quarter by `model.rotation` (n = 4) and solved by `solve.frequency` in three
harmonics on the same mesh: m = 1 passes the rotating TE11+ (e^{−jφ}), m = 0 the TM01, m = 2
the TE21+ (its counter-rotating TE21− beside it in the same harmonic). The ports take the
circle's rotating modes in closed form; each transmission is compared with e^{−jβL}.
