# Stripline ends

A strip of no thickness, 4 mm wide, midway between planes 2 mm apart in PTFE (εr = 2.2), runs
10 mm from the port into a closed box and stops: first open, then on a via, a conducting post
from the bottom plane to the top across the strip's end and 2 mm past its edges. The line's TEM
mode is even about the strip's plane, so only the half below it is meshed, a magnetic wall
closing it there with the strip a conductor on that wall.

With β = k₀√εr exact, the reflection's phase is the line's length and the end's: the via is a
short at its face; the open end reflects as if the strip went on a little further, by Δ. In the
limit of a wide strip Δ = b ln 2/π (0.441 mm here), the same excess width Cohn's mapping gives
each side edge; for a strip twice as wide as the spacing the end's corners take some of that
away, so the reference takes the middle of a bracket between the end's fringing over the
strip's width alone and the wide-strip limit, and the tolerance is the bracket's half-width.
The model's Δ converges to 0.397 mm, near the bracket's middle (0.401 mm): 137.533°, 137.763°
and 137.791° with 0.5 mm cells (a fifth of that at the strip's edges, as here), and 0.35 and
0.25 mm (a tenth); the via's short is within 0.03° of ideal.
