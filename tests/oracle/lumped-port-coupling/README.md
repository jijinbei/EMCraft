# Lumped-port coupling

Three lumped ports, each a 1 mm gap along z, in a 60 mm box lined with a 15 mm perfectly matched
layer at 10 GHz: the first driven by a current source (1 GV behind 1 GΩ: 1 A to 1e-8), the
others open (1 GΩ, no source), one 15 mm to its side and one 15 mm along its axis. The gaps'
currents are uniform, so each is a Hertzian dipole and everything has a closed form: the driven
gap's radiation resistance, and the voltages the open gaps see — the mutual impedances — from
the exact near and far field of its current. This checks the port's current, its voltage, their
coupling through the mesh and the layer together.
