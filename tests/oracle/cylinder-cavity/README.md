# Cylindrical cavity

A closed cylinder of radius 10 mm and length 30 mm, meshed with unstructured tetrahedra by the
tetrahedral mesher (boundary recovery, refinement, smoothing, the boundary vertices put on the
exact cylinder and planes), the tetrahedra on the wall curved: their edges' midpoints moved onto
the cylinder, the elements mapped quadratically. Its three lowest resonances — the TE111 pair
and TM010 — against the closed form with the tabulated Bessel zeros: within 4e-5, where flat
facets inscribed in the wall would make the cavity smaller by an O(h²) 0.4 %.
