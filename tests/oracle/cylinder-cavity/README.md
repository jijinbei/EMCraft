# Cylindrical cavity

A closed cylinder of radius 10 mm and length 30 mm, meshed with unstructured tetrahedra by the
tetrahedral mesher (boundary recovery, refinement, smoothing, the boundary vertices put on the
exact cylinder and planes). Its three lowest resonances — the TE111 pair and TM010 — against the
closed form with the tabulated Bessel zeros. The flat facets inscribed in the curved wall make
the cavity slightly smaller: the error is geometric, O(h²), and falls to 0.25 % at 2 mm.
