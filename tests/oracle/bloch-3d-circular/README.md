# A straight cell's Bloch waves, solved in 3D

One 16 mm cell of a circular guide (radius 10 mm) with a port on each end, four modes each,
solved in 3D (`solve.bloch`): the cell's generalized scattering matrix between all eight modes,
the far port's modes written in the near port's basis, and the transfer matrix's eigenvalues.
A straight guide's Bloch waves are its own modes: TE11 in both polarizations and TM01 (decaying
below its cut-off at 11.47 GHz, propagating at 13 GHz), against the closed forms.
