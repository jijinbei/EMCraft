# Stripline

A strip of no thickness, 1.5 mm wide, across the middle of a 6 × 3 mm box filled with PTFE
(εr = 2.2), 6 mm long. The strip ends on both ports, so their cross-sections hold it as a
conductor and their modes come from the cross-section solver: TEM first, at β = k0 √εr exactly
(two conductors in one dielectric). The mesh closes in on the strip's edges, where the field is
singular, to a twentieth of the size.
