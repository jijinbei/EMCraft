# Hertzian dipole

A 1 mm current element of 1 A at the centre of a 60 mm box of vacuum, every face lined with a
15 mm perfectly matched layer (half a wavelength at 10 GHz), meshed at six second-order elements
per wavelength. The power through a Huygens box between the dipole and the layer, and the
directivity from the near-to-far transform of that box's equivalent currents, against the closed
forms. The dipole sits slightly off the mesh's symmetry so it lies inside one tetrahedron.
