# Conducting sphere scattering

A plane wave on a perfectly conducting sphere of radius 5 mm (ka = 1.05, near the peak of the
sphere's back-scatter) at 10 GHz, in a 50 mm box lined with a 12 mm perfectly matched layer.
The solver works with the scattered field, whose tangential trace on the conductor is
−n × E_inc: those boundary values are the L² projection of the incident field onto the
surface's edge functions, lifted into the right-hand side element by element. Scattering
cross-section and bistatic radar cross-section against the Mie series of the conducting sphere.
