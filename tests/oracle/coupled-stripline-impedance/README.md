# Coupled stripline impedances

Two strips of no thickness side by side, a narrow gap apart, midway between two ground planes in
one dielectric, the shielding box's side walls far enough to leave the planes nearly infinite.
Both TEM modes travel at the speed of light in the fill, so the cross-section solver returns
some pair of them; the port splits that pair into the line's own modes, the ones whose currents
on the two strips are orthogonal: the even mode (equal currents) and the odd mode (opposite
currents). Each mode's impedance per strip, its power over the strips' currents, is compared
with Cohn's conformal mapping of coupled strips. The odd mode's impedance is the lower: its
field crowds into the gap.
