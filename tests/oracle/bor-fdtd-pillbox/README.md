# Pillbox cavity rung in the time domain as a body of revolution

The vacuum pillbox of radius 10 mm and length 15 mm on its meridian half-plane's (r, z) Yee
grid in 0.25 mm cells (its walls on grid lines), rung by `solve.revolution.time_domain` in
orders m = 0 and 1: the ring-down's spectral peaks against the closed forms. The frequency
domain's solve of the same cavity is the `bor-pillbox` case.
