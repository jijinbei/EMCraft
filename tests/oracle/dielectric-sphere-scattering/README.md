# Dielectric sphere scattering

A plane wave of 1 V/m, travelling along +z with E along x, on a ceramic sphere (εr = 4, radius
5 mm) at 10 GHz. The box around it (50 mm) is lined with a 12 mm perfectly matched layer, the
mesh made at six second-order elements per wavelength in each material, the sphere's surface
curved. The solver finds the scattered field (the plane wave is the source of the field the
dielectric scatters), the power it carries through a Huygens box, and the bistatic radar
cross-section from the near-to-far transform — all against the Mie series.
