# Time-domain gaps

The time domain's open problem: two 1 mm lumped gaps 15 mm apart in a 60 mm box with a 15 mm
CPML on every face, on a uniform Yee grid of 1 mm cells, the first driven (a current source:
1 GV behind 1 GΩ), the second open. A Gaussian-modulated pulse; the gaps' voltages and currents
and the fields on a Huygens box Fourier-transformed at 10 GHz. Held to the same closed forms as
the frequency-domain case `lumped-port-coupling` — and closer to them (no singular field on an
element mesh: the Yee grid's gaps are its own edges).
