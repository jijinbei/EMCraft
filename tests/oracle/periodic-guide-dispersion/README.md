# Periodic guide dispersion

WR-90 (22.86 × 10.16 mm) cut into a 10 mm period whose faces z = 0 and z = 10 mm are tied
by the Floquet condition: the eigenfrequencies at ψ = 0°, 90° and 180° per period are the
empty guide's modes with β = (ψ + 2πq)/L. Band 1 is TE10's dispersion f = c/2π √((π/a)² + β²),
band 2 TE20 at ψ = 0° and 90° and, at the zone's edge, TE10's backward harmonic, degenerate
with band 1.
