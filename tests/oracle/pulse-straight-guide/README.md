# A pulse through a straight circular guide, from one cell's Bloch wave

One 16 mm cell of a circular guide (radius 10 mm), its ports on its two ends: its TE11 Bloch wave
on the half-plane (`solve.revolution.bloch`, the transfer matrix of the cell's generalized
scattering matrix with three modes on each end), and a 2 ns pulse at 12.5 GHz through a metre of
it (`results.pulse` with `length`). A straight guide's Bloch wave is its own TE11 wave, so the
cell's β, the pulse's group delay L/v_g and its slope are the closed forms of the guide.
