# E-plane step

WR-90 (22.86 × 10.16 mm) stepping down to half its height (5.08 mm) against the y = 0 wall:
an E-plane discontinuity, where the TE10 wave couples to the TE_1n and TM_1n modes — together,
the LSE^x_1n modes that share its sin(πx/a). Solved by the frequency-domain FEM (graded towards
the step's edge) and by the time-domain solver, both against mode matching of the LSE modes
with 640 modes. The ports have different heights, so the S-parameters are power waves.
