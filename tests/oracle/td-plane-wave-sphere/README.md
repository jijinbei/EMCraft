# Plane waves on spheres, time domain

A plane wave along z, polarised along x, on a sphere of radius 5 mm in the open box, solved by
the time-domain solver's total-field/scattered-field split: the incident wave from a
one-dimensional grid along z with the same cells, step and CPML, corrected onto the faces of a
box round the sphere, the Huygens box outside it in the scattered field. A faint sphere (εr
1.0001) checks the split — no incident wave leaks out of it — and a ceramic one (εr 4) the whole
of it, both against the Mie series.
