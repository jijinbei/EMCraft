# Slab-loaded guide

WR-90 with a polystyrene layer (εr = 2.25) filling its lower half across the whole width, 20 mm
long, its ports at the ends. The layered cross-section has no closed-form modes in the code, so
its ports take theirs from the cross-section solver: the dominant one is LSM₁₀, TE10-like but
hybrid (an axial electric field). Collin's transcendental equation gives its β exactly, solved
independently by bisection; the port mode's effective permittivity and the transmission's phase
are compared with it.
