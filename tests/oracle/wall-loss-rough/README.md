# Rough wall losses

The [wall-loss](../wall-loss/) guide, 200 mm of WR-90 with copper walls at 10 GHz, with walls of
1 µm rms roughness. The skin depth is 0.661 µm, so the roughness is larger than it and the
Hammerstad–Jensen factor K = 1 + (2/π) arctan(1.4 (Δ/δ)²) = 1.8075 multiplies the surface
impedance. The reference is the smooth TE10 attenuation 0.01248 Np/m times K, 0.02255 Np/m:
the guide loses 0.0392 dB, against the smooth guide's 0.0217 dB.
