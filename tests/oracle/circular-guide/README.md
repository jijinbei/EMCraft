# Circular waveguide

A 30 mm length of empty circular guide, radius 10 mm, meshed with unstructured tetrahedra, its
ports closed by the circular guide's modes — the TE11 pair (both polarizations) and TM01, from
the Bessel functions and their zeros. The excited TE11 must pass with the phase βL of the closed
form and not reflect. The error is the geometry's: flat facets inscribed in the curved wall
narrow the guide and raise its cut-off; it falls as O(h²).
