# Loaded periodic guide

WR-90 loaded periodically with polystyrene (εr = 2.56): each 10 mm period holds 5 mm of vacuum
and 5 mm of dielectric filling the cross-section, the faces z = 0 and z = 10 mm tied by the
Floquet condition. Sections that fill the cross-section keep TE10 alone, so its Bloch band is the
closed form cos ψ = (A + D)/2 of the period's transmission-line ABCD matrix; the lowest band at
ψ = 45°, 90° and 135° against its roots.
