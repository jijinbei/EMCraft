# Hertzian dipole in an open body of revolution

A 1 mm current element (1 A) along z at the centre of a vacuum sphere of radius 60 mm whose
outer 20 mm are a spherical perfectly matched layer, at 3 GHz (λ = 100 mm), solved on the
meridian half-plane in order m = 0 by `solve.revolution.radiation`: the power out through the
Huygens surface (turned about the axis) and the directivity broadside, against the closed form.
The 3D solve of the same problem is the `dipole-radiation-spherical-pml` case.
