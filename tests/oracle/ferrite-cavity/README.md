# Ferrite-filled cavity

A 22.86 × 10.16 × 30 mm box filled with a ferrite like YIG (εr = 13, 4πMs = 1780 G), saturated
by an internal field of 3000 Oe across its height. Filled whole, its TE101 field keeps its
sine shapes and sees the Polder tensor's effective permeability μe = (μ² − κ²)/μ, which itself
depends on the frequency: the resonance solves k₀² εr μe(f) = (π/a)² + (π/d)², 1.8014 GHz. A
small lumped probe in a corner sees the resonance as a dip in |S11|, its bottom found by a
parabola through the sweep's least points.
