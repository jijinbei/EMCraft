# Pillbox cavity on a quarter

The closed cylinder of `bor-pillbox` (radius 10 mm, length 15 mm) meshed on one quarter by
`model.rotation` (n = 4) and solved per harmonic by `solve.cyclic`: the two cut faces tied by
e^{−j2πm/4}, so m = 0 gives the TM01p family and m = 1 TE111 and TM110, as the whole would.
Each mode is compared with the closed form; the whole cavity in 3D is the `cylinder-cavity`
case.
