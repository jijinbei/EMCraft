# Temperature of a lossy dielectric

30 mm of WR-90 filled with a low-loss dielectric (εr = 2.2, tan δ = 0.002, thermal
conductivity 0.25 W/(m·K)), its ports in the filling so it is matched, 100 W at 10 GHz. The
line absorbs 2.06 W, deposited with the TE10 field's square, q₀ sin²(πx/a). With the walls a
heat sink at 20 °C the temperature solves −k∇²T = q on the guide's cross-section with T = 0 on
its sides; its double sine series gives a rise of 23.2 K at the centre. `results.thermal` solves
the 3D heat conduction with linear elements on the mesh.
