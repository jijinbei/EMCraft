# Lossy slab-loaded guide

WR-90 with a lossy dielectric layer (εr = 2.25, tan δ = 0.02) filling its lower half across the
whole width, 20 mm long, its ports at the ends. Each port's cross-section holds air and the lossy
layer, so its modes are hybrid and lossy: the dominant one is LSM₁₀ with a complex propagation
constant γ = α + jβ. Collin's transcendental equation for the slab-loaded guide holds with the
complex permittivity; solved independently in complex arithmetic (the lossless root followed as
the loss grows), it gives γ. The port mode's −Re γ²/k0² and attenuation and the line's S21 are
compared with it — the lossy twin of the `slab-loaded-guide` case.
