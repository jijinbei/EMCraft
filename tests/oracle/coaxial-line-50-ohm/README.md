# Coaxial line referred to 50 Ω

The coaxial line of `coaxial-line` (Z₀ = 51.8 Ω), its ports referred to 50 Ω. The ports' modes
come from the cross-section solver, which also gives the line's impedance by power and current;
S of the modes' power waves is then referred to 50 Ω through each port's voltage and current. A
uniform mismatched line between two 50 Ω references has S in closed form.
