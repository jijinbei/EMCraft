# Roadmap

EMCraft's goal is electromagnetic simulation on par with CST Studio Suite and HFSS for microwave
and RF components, open, verified and scriptable. Each milestone lands with oracle cases against
exact solutions or published benchmarks.

| Milestone | Scope | Status |
|---|---|---|
| M0 Foundation | Workspace, quality gates (layers, no-panic lints), SI model with display units, command engine with rollback and panic guard, scripts, CLI | done |
| M1 Waveguide high-pass filter | Structured tetrahedral meshes of rectangular waveguides in sections; Nédélec elements of orders 1 and 2 with exact element matrices; modal waveguide ports (TE and TM modes); multifrontal complex symmetric LDLᵀ with Bunch–Kaufman pivots, nested dissection, relaxed supernodes, blocked threaded fronts; power-normalised S-parameters; E and H fields; closed-form TE10 references; oracle cases (empty guide, dielectric step, high-pass filter) with fixtures; convergence-rate tests; field pictures (H-plane, E-plane, cross-section) and S-parameter charts as PNG; egui desktop app with phase animation | done |
| M2 Discontinuities | H-plane steps and inductive irises (sections with their own aperture across x, nesting from one to the next): meshes without the cells outside an aperture, graded geometrically towards re-entrant edges, port modes on offset apertures, power waves between ports of different aperture (done); mode matching with generalised scattering matrices as an independent reference (done); iris-coupled band-pass filter sample and oracle cases (done); E-plane steps and capacitive irises (apertures across y, LSE^x_1n mode matching, FEM and time domain against it) (done); posts, partially overlapping apertures, steps in both width and height at once, comparison with published filter designs | in progress |
| M3 General geometry | Tetrahedral meshing of arbitrary bodies (from SolidCraft, through FieldCraft's mesher), curved boundaries, size fields per wavelength | planned |
| M4 Materials and losses | Lossy dielectrics (tan δ) in the model, solver and references, the discrete Poynting balance checked (done); finite-conductivity walls by the Leontovich surface-impedance condition, checked against the TE10 conductor attenuation and the wall-loss balance (done); Q factors (done with M5); ports in lossy media, dispersive materials, magnetic losses | in progress |
| M5 Eigenmodes | Cavity resonances by shift-invert subspace iteration (`solve.eigenmodes`, the gradient null space kept away by the shift), Q factors of walls and dielectrics by perturbation, mode pictures, `cavity` sample and oracle case (done); null-space deflation (tree–cotree) for modes far above the shift, lossy eigenproblems (complex frequencies), port mode solver for arbitrary cross-sections | in progress |
| M6 Time domain | Finite integration on tensor (non-uniform) hexahedral grids, threaded with deterministic results; TE10 pulse in PML-terminated feeds, broadband S-parameters by on-the-fly Fourier transforms against a uniform-guide reference run; checked against the exact cascade (second-order convergence) and mode matching (done); losses, multi-mode ports, conformal (sub-cell) edges, field monitors and animation | in progress |
| M7 Radiation | Absorbing boundaries and PML, far fields, antennas | planned |
