# Roadmap

EMCraft's goal is electromagnetic simulation on par with CST Studio Suite and HFSS for microwave
and RF components, open, verified and scriptable. Each milestone lands with oracle cases against
exact solutions or published benchmarks.

| Milestone | Scope | Status |
|---|---|---|
| M0 Foundation | Workspace, quality gates (layers, no-panic lints), SI model with display units, command engine with rollback and panic guard, scripts, CLI | done |

Junction templates (done): `h-bend-radius`, `e-bend-radius`, `e-tee`, `magic-tee` (matched by posts in its H and E arms, narrow band) and `cross-guide-coupler` (two guides crossed over a common wall, two holes); engine tests of losslessness, reciprocity and their symmetries (the `e-tee` also against the oracle's `eplane-tee`). Junction sweeps faster (done): each frequency the fast sweep solves in full adds three moments of its fields (A⁻¹ M x, …: multi-point moment matching, Slone–Lee–Lee 2001), its reduced matrices grown with the basis rather than formed at each frequency, the excitations sharing each pass over the factor (a matrix product per supernode), and nested dissection keeping the smallest separator of planes across every axis near the median (the magic tee's root front 11 168 → 3 958 rows, its factorization 1.0 → 0.19 Tflop): the junction templates' 101 points take 2–3 factorizations instead of 6–9, S within 3e-9 of before on the same mesh; magic tee 757 s and 9.4 GB → 158 s and 4.4 GB, cross-guide coupler 126 → 43 s, `h-bend-radius` 112 → 21 s, `h-tee` 13 → 5 s, `e-tee` 8 → 3 s (release, 4 threads). Next: the other junctions against published references where one holds them to a tolerance; symmetry planes for solids (a tee's mirror plane: the half meshed, solved with an electric and a magnetic wall, the mirrored ports' S combined from the two, about halving each factorization's work and memory again); the magic tee's posts, whose fine mesh (about 40 000 tetrahedra whatever the element size) sets its size | in progress |
