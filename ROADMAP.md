# Roadmap

EMCraft's goal is electromagnetic simulation on par with CST Studio Suite and HFSS for microwave
and RF components, open, verified and scriptable. Each milestone lands with oracle cases against
exact solutions or published benchmarks.

| Milestone | Scope | Status |
|---|---|---|
| M0 Foundation | Workspace, quality gates (layers, no-panic lints), SI model with display units, command engine with rollback and panic guard, scripts, CLI | done |
| M1 Waveguide high-pass filter | Structured tetrahedral meshes of rectangular waveguides in sections; Nédélec elements of orders 1 and 2 with exact element matrices; modal waveguide ports (TE and TM modes); multifrontal complex symmetric LDLᵀ with Bunch–Kaufman pivots, nested dissection, relaxed supernodes, blocked threaded fronts; power-normalised S-parameters; E and H fields; closed-form TE10 references; oracle cases (empty guide, dielectric step, high-pass filter) with fixtures; convergence-rate tests; field pictures (H-plane, E-plane, cross-section) and S-parameter charts as PNG; egui desktop app with phase animation | done |
| M2 Discontinuities | H-plane steps and inductive irises (sections with their own aperture across x, nesting from one to the next): meshes without the cells outside an aperture, graded geometrically towards re-entrant edges, port modes on offset apertures, power waves between ports of different aperture (done); mode matching with generalised scattering matrices as an independent reference (done); iris-coupled band-pass filter sample and oracle cases (done); E-plane steps and capacitive irises (apertures across y, LSE^x_1n mode matching, FEM and time domain against it) (done); posts, as Boolean features of an unstructured guide (M3) (done); partially overlapping apertures, steps in both width and height at once, comparison with published filter designs | in progress |
| M3 General geometry | The unstructured tetrahedral mesher ported from FieldCraft into `emcraft-tetmesh` (adaptive predicates, surface checks, Delaunay, constrained boundary recovery, refinement, smoothing, improvement; dielectric and conducting inclusions: each material region meshed against a frozen interface, the points boundary recovery adds passed between the sides until they agree, the regions joined node for node, checked against the two-layer spherical cavity (done); circular-waveguide ports with the TE/TM Bessel modes in both polarizations, checked against the TE11 phase (done); curved elements — the edges on curved walls and interfaces bent onto them, quadratic maps with the covariant Piola transform — taking the curved bodies' error from the O(h²) of flat facets (5.6e-3 at 3 mm for the cylinder) to 4e-5 (done); Boolean combinations of primitives with the body (`geometry.feature`: add, subtract, intersect; boxes, cylinders and spheres along any axis) by a mesh Boolean on exact predicates, flush faces moved past each other first, checked against mode matching on an iris made of two subtracted plates (done); imported surfaces — STL (binary, ASCII) and 3MF read by `emcraft-io`, grouped into faces by angle, ports on their end planes — checked against mode matching on a step read from STL (done); element sizes from the wavelength in each material (`per_wavelength`), each region of a solid at its own (done); a faster quality stage with the same mesh bit for bit (the quality objective on shared edge vectors, skin-index entries carrying boxes, Klincsek's table with a floor, the sandwiched face found directly, smoothing reusing its evaluations): the 3 mm post through WR-90 at 4 mm meshes in about 3.2 s instead of 5 s (`post_mesh_timing`) (done) | done |
| M4 Materials and losses | Dispersive materials (done): Debye, Lorentz and Drude poles (`model.material.create` `debye`, `lorentz`, `drude`), exact ε(ω) per frequency in the FEM and its fast sweep (each pole's mass matrix projected apart), auxiliary differential equations in the time domain (Taflove & Hagness ch. 9), oracle `dispersive-slab` against an independent ABCD cascade; lossy dielectrics (tan δ) in the model, solver and references, the discrete Poynting balance checked (done); finite-conductivity walls by the Leontovich surface-impedance condition, checked against the TE10 conductor attenuation and the wall-loss balance (done); Q factors (done with M5); magnetic losses (done): `magnetic_loss_tangent`, μ = μr(1 − j tan δm), the curl–curl matrix complex, H, the absorbed power ½ωμ₀∫μ″|H|² (the discrete Poynting balance to 1e-9) and Q_m from the complex frequency (shared with Q_d by their powers), refused by the time domain; oracles `magnetic-loss-line` and `cavity-magnetic-loss` (Q_m = 1/(2 tan(½ atan tan δm)) exactly); ports in lossy and dispersive media (done): a port filled with one lossy or dispersive material takes its modes' complex γ = α + jβ and κ (closed forms; numerically solved cross-sections by a unit filling, TE/TEM told from TM by the scalar part), S read as pseudo-waves referred to each port's wave impedance, reciprocal (Marks & Williams 1992), de-embedding by the complex γ; oracles `lossy-line` (|S21| = e^{−αL}, arg −βL, S11 ≈ 1e-4) and `lossy-step` (not yet: a port across several materials one of which is lossy — hybrid lossy modes, refused —, the complex impedance of a lossy TEM line for reference impedances, losses in the time domain) | done |
| M5 Eigenmodes | Cavity resonances by shift-invert subspace iteration (`solve.eigenmodes`, the gradient null space kept away by the shift), Q factors of walls and dielectrics by perturbation, mode pictures, `cavity` sample and oracle case (done); port modes of any cross-section — Lee–Sun–Cendes edge/nodal elements on the port's triangles (curved with the volume's), shift-invert subspace iteration on the constraint that removes the β = 0 family, nonsymmetric projected problems by Francis QR — checked against the empty and slab-loaded rectangular guide (LSE/LSM, Collin) and a coaxial line's TEM; ports take them wherever the closed forms do not apply, the port condition generalised to hybrid modes, its rank-one terms bordering the matrix instead of filling it; `coaxial-line` sample and oracle case (done); shielded planar circuits as guides — dielectric layers across the box and strips of no thickness, the mesh closing in on strips' edges, the ports' cross-sections holding them — checked against the slab-loaded guide's LSM₁₀ (Collin), a stripline's TEM and Hammerstad–Jensen's microstrip ε_eff; `solve.port_modes`; `slab-loaded-guide`, `stripline` and `microstrip-eps-eff` oracle cases (done); port impedances by power and current (the conductor's current in weak form) and S referred to reference impedances (`impedance` on a port), checked against the coaxial line's Z₀, Cohn's stripline and Hammerstad–Jensen's microstrip; `coaxial-line-50-ohm`, `stripline-impedance` oracle cases (done); de-embedding: each port's reference plane moved along its line by its first mode's γ, S′ = S e^{γ_p d_p + γ_q d_q}, `results.compare` moved alike (done, oracle `deembedding`); the discrete gradients projected out of the eigen subspace (Arbenz–Geus), so modes far above the shift come too (done); lossy cavities by complex frequencies — complex symmetric subspace iteration, a dense complex QR for the projected problem, Q_d = Re ω / 2 Im ω exactly (done); strips ending inside the box (open stubs, a stub branching off a line) and vias (`vias` on `geometry.waveguide`: conducting posts, their cells left out, faces graded), verified against transmission-line theory — the open end's extension and a via's short (`stripline-ends`), open and via-shorted stub notches within 0.5 % (`stripline-stub`), on the half below a magnetic wall through the strips' plane (done); coupled-line filters (done): strips side by side and offset along z (parallel-coupled sections), refused when they overlap or leave a gap no mesh resolves; the degenerate TEM modes of several strips split along the line's impedance matrix (power-orthogonal, orthogonal conductor currents: the even and odd modes), each mode's impedance per conductor 2P/Σ|I|², halves under symmetry planes counted per conductor; `strip_end` grading on `mesh.generate`; checked against Cohn's coupled-strip mapping (Z0e, Z0o within 0.3 %, `coupled-stripline-impedance`), the open-circuited coupled section's inverter (`coupled-line-coupler`) and a three-resonator 10 % Chebyshev filter designed by Cohn's and Matthaei–Young–Jones' equations against the ideal TEM cascade (band edges within 0.45 %, `coupled-line-bandpass`); `coupled-filter` sample (done) | in progress |
| M6 Time domain | Finite integration on tensor (non-uniform) hexahedral grids, threaded with deterministic results; TE10 pulse in PML-terminated feeds, broadband S-parameters by on-the-fly Fourier transforms against a uniform-guide reference run; checked against the exact cascade (second-order convergence) and mode matching (done); losses, multi-mode ports, conformal (sub-cell) edges, field monitors and animation | in progress |
| M7 Radiation | A box lined with a perfectly matched layer (complex coordinate stretching as an anisotropic medium, frequency-scaled for one reflection at every frequency) (done); a current element and plane waves on dielectric bodies (the scattered field) as sources (done); lumped ports (`model.port.lumped`: a gap, its current along a line or round a wire's surface, a source behind an impedance) with input and active impedances, S11 and the power balance, checked against the exact mutual impedances of short gaps and a half-wave wire dipole's directivity (done); the factor out of core on disk past a memory budget (done); waveguide ports on faces inside a body (`face`), with the open boundary: antennas fed by a guide, their S11 swept and their accepted power against the radiated (done); arrays of lumped ports driven together with phases, their active impedances and patterns checked against the exact array of Hertzian dipoles; the Huygens box placed between the sources and the layer (done); open problems in the time domain — a uniform Yee grid of the box with a CPML on all faces, current elements and lumped ports (resistive voltage sources), on-the-fly transforms of the ports and a Huygens box — checked against the same Hertzian closed forms (done); spherical layers (`model.open` `shape` sphere): a sphere solid's outer shell stretched along r (Teixeira–Chew tensors at quadrature points), its own mesh layer, a Huygens sphere; the Hertzian dipole within 0.8 % in power and 1–3 % in directivity with about half the box layer's unknowns at the same element size (oracle `dipole-radiation-spherical-pml`) (done) | in progress |
| M8 Performance | Timings by stage in every result; one symbolic factorization per sweep (done); a fast frequency sweep by reduced-basis projection, the worst-resolved frequency solved in full until every residual is within 1e-4 — a 41-point coaxial sweep 144 → 28 s, S within 1e-10 of full solves (done); the fronts' block updates and the panels' rank-one and rank-two updates by faer's matrix products (SIMD chosen at run time, thread-independent sums), 64-column blocks — the coaxial line's numeric factorization 3.0 → 1.9 s (done); an extruded mesher for planar circuits was tried and dropped: the structured grid's long thin cells along strips' edges already suit their line singularity, and the extrusion took more tetrahedra; light subtrees of the supernodal tree on threads (the factor the same bit for bit), the ports' terms made once per frequency in a fast sweep — the coaxial line's 41 points: 144 s at the start, 79 s solving each in full, 14 s swept fast (done); the block's L used in place and W column-major, the panels' updates threaded on large fronts — 1.35 → 1.24 s; single precision tried and dropped (faer's complex f32 products are no faster here, so it would buy only memory); symmetry planes (done): `model.symmetry` puts an electric or magnetic wall on x = a/2 or y = b/2, only the half or quarter meshed, ports with the modes of the kept symmetry, fields beyond the plane by images — the iris band-pass filter's half matches the whole's mode matching (oracle `symmetry-half-filter`) with 54 912 of 108 974 unknowns, 1.0 s instead of 2.4 s; adaptive refinement (done): `mesh.adapt` runs passes until ΔS < tolerance — a residual indicator (element residual plus jumps of tangential μ⁻¹∇×E and normal εE; Monk; Beck et al.), Dörfler marking and graded remeshing for solids, uniform h ÷ 1.4 for waveguide grids; oracle `adaptive-iris` four times closer than uniform refinement at the same unknowns, `adaptive-post` converging to an independent Nyström reference within the curved surface's 1e-4 geometric floor; the solvers on GPUs (done): kernels in Rust through CubeCL (ADR 0002), `emcraft-gpu` — the whole time domain (the guide with dispersive edges, the open box with lumped ports, current elements and the Huygens box's transforms) in single or double precision, the high-pass filter at 0.5 mm 70 s → 5.6 s on an RTX 3080, single within 1e-6 of the threads; the factorization's large fronts held by the device in complex double, the host pivoting each block, about the CPU's pace on a consumer card (`solve.device`, `solve.devices`); next: wgpu (Vulkan, Metal), the fronts' pivoting on the device | in progress |
