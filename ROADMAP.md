# Roadmap

EMCraft's goal is electromagnetic simulation on par with CST Studio Suite and HFSS for microwave
and RF components, open, verified and scriptable. Each milestone lands with oracle cases against
exact solutions or published benchmarks.

| Milestone | Scope | Status |
|---|---|---|
| M0 Foundation | Workspace, quality gates (layers, no-panic lints), SI model with display units, command engine with rollback and panic guard, scripts, CLI | done |
| M1 Waveguide high-pass filter | Structured tetrahedral meshes of rectangular waveguides in sections; Nédélec elements of orders 1 and 2 with exact element matrices; modal waveguide ports (TE and TM modes); multifrontal complex symmetric LDLᵀ with Bunch–Kaufman pivots, nested dissection, relaxed supernodes, blocked threaded fronts; power-normalised S-parameters; E and H fields; closed-form TE10 references; oracle cases (empty guide, dielectric step, high-pass filter) with fixtures; convergence-rate tests; field pictures (H-plane, E-plane, cross-section) and S-parameter charts as PNG; egui desktop app with phase animation | done |
| M2 Discontinuities | H-plane steps and inductive irises (sections with their own aperture across x, nesting from one to the next): meshes without the cells outside an aperture, graded geometrically towards re-entrant edges, port modes on offset apertures, power waves between ports of different aperture (done); mode matching with generalised scattering matrices as an independent reference (done); iris-coupled band-pass filter sample and oracle cases (done); E-plane steps and capacitive irises (apertures across y, LSE^x_1n mode matching, FEM and time domain against it) (done); posts, as Boolean features of an unstructured guide (M3) (done); partially overlapping apertures, steps in both width and height at once, comparison with published filter designs | in progress |
| M3 General geometry | The unstructured tetrahedral mesher ported from FieldCraft into `emcraft-tetmesh` (adaptive predicates, surface checks, Delaunay, constrained boundary recovery, refinement, smoothing, improvement; its 126 tests) (done); primitive bodies — box, cylinder, sphere — tessellated with exact surfaces and meshed, bucket point location, eigenmodes and driven solves on them, checked against Bessel-zero resonances and the TE10 guide (done); dielectric and conducting inclusions: each material region meshed against a frozen interface, the points boundary recovery adds passed between the sides until they agree, the regions joined node for node, checked against the two-layer spherical cavity (done); circular-waveguide ports with the TE/TM Bessel modes in both polarizations, checked against the TE11 phase (done); curved elements — the edges on curved walls and interfaces bent onto them, quadratic maps with the covariant Piola transform — taking the curved bodies' error from the O(h²) of flat facets (5.6e-3 at 3 mm for the cylinder) to 4e-5 (done); Boolean combinations of primitives with the body (`geometry.feature`: add, subtract, intersect; boxes, cylinders and spheres along any axis) by a mesh Boolean on exact predicates, flush faces moved past each other first, checked against mode matching on an iris made of two subtracted plates (done); imported surfaces — STL (binary, ASCII) and 3MF read by `emcraft-io`, grouped into faces by angle, ports on their end planes — checked against mode matching on a step read from STL (done); element sizes from the wavelength in each material (`per_wavelength`), each region of a solid at its own (done); next: a faster quality stage (the improvement's vertex insertions take 4.5 of the 5 s that a 3 mm post through WR-90 meshes in at 4 mm, 11 000 tetrahedra) | done |
| M4 Materials and losses | Dispersive materials (done): Debye, Lorentz and Drude poles (`model.material.create` `debye`, `lorentz`, `drude`), exact ε(ω) per frequency in the FEM and its fast sweep (each pole's mass matrix projected apart), auxiliary differential equations in the time domain (Taflove & Hagness ch. 9), oracle `dispersive-slab` against an independent ABCD cascade; lossy dielectrics (tan δ) in the model, solver and references, the discrete Poynting balance checked (done); finite-conductivity walls by the Leontovich surface-impedance condition, checked against the TE10 conductor attenuation and the wall-loss balance (done); Q factors (done with M5); ports in lossy media, dispersive materials, magnetic losses | in progress |
| M5 Eigenmodes | Cavity resonances by shift-invert subspace iteration (`solve.eigenmodes`, the gradient null space kept away by the shift), Q factors of walls and dielectrics by perturbation, mode pictures, `cavity` sample and oracle case (done); port modes of any cross-section — Lee–Sun–Cendes edge/nodal elements on the port's triangles (curved with the volume's), shift-invert subspace iteration on the constraint that removes the β = 0 family, nonsymmetric projected problems by Francis QR — checked against the empty and slab-loaded rectangular guide (LSE/LSM, Collin) and a coaxial line's TEM; ports take them wherever the closed forms do not apply, the port condition generalised to hybrid modes, its rank-one terms bordering the matrix instead of filling it; `coaxial-line` sample and oracle case (done); shielded planar circuits as guides — dielectric layers across the box and strips of no thickness, the mesh closing in on strips' edges, the ports' cross-sections holding them — checked against the slab-loaded guide's LSM₁₀ (Collin), a stripline's TEM and Hammerstad–Jensen's microstrip ε_eff; `solve.port_modes`; `slab-loaded-guide`, `stripline` and `microstrip-eps-eff` oracle cases (done); port impedances by power and current (the conductor's current in weak form) and S referred to reference impedances (`impedance` on a port), checked against the coaxial line's Z₀, Cohn's stripline and Hammerstad–Jensen's microstrip; `coaxial-line-50-ohm`, `stripline-impedance` oracle cases (done); de-embedding: each port's reference plane moved along its line by its first mode's γ, S′ = S e^{γ_p d_p + γ_q d_q}, `results.compare` moved alike (done, oracle `deembedding`); the discrete gradients projected out of the eigen subspace (Arbenz–Geus), so modes far above the shift come too (done); lossy cavities by complex frequencies — complex symmetric subspace iteration, a dense complex QR for the projected problem, Q_d = Re ω / 2 Im ω exactly (done); next: strips with ends inside the box (stubs, coupled-line filters) verified, vias | in progress |
| M6 Time domain | Finite integration on tensor (non-uniform) hexahedral grids, threaded with deterministic results; TE10 pulse in PML-terminated feeds, broadband S-parameters by on-the-fly Fourier transforms against a uniform-guide reference run; checked against the exact cascade (second-order convergence) and mode matching (done); losses, multi-mode ports, conformal (sub-cell) edges, field monitors and animation | in progress |
| M7 Radiation | A box lined with a perfectly matched layer (complex coordinate stretching as an anisotropic medium, frequency-scaled for one reflection at every frequency) (done); a current element and plane waves on dielectric bodies (the scattered field) as sources (done); far fields by the near-to-far transform of a Huygens box, radiated and scattered power, directivity and radar cross-section, pattern charts (done); checked against the Hertzian dipole and the Mie series (done); plane waves on conductors, the scattered field's boundary values n × E_s = −n × E_inc projected on their surfaces and lifted into the right-hand side, checked against the conducting sphere's Mie series (done); lumped ports (`model.port.lumped`: a gap, its current along a line or round a wire's surface, a source behind an impedance) with input and active impedances, S11 and the power balance, checked against the exact mutual impedances of short gaps and a half-wave wire dipole's directivity (done); the factor out of core on disk past a memory budget (done); waveguide ports on faces inside a body (`face`), with the open boundary: antennas fed by a guide, their S11 swept and their accepted power against the radiated (done); arrays of lumped ports driven together with phases, their active impedances and patterns checked against the exact array of Hertzian dipoles; the Huygens box placed between the sources and the layer (done); open problems in the time domain — a uniform Yee grid of the box with a CPML on all faces, current elements and lumped ports (resistive voltage sources), on-the-fly transforms of the ports and a Huygens box — checked against the same Hertzian closed forms (done); next: spherical or conformal layers | in progress |
| M8 Performance | Timings by stage in every result; one symbolic factorization per sweep (done); a fast frequency sweep by reduced-basis projection, the worst-resolved frequency solved in full until every residual is within 1e-4 — a 41-point coaxial sweep 144 → 28 s, S within 1e-10 of full solves (done); the fronts' block updates and the panels' rank-one and rank-two updates by faer's matrix products (SIMD chosen at run time, thread-independent sums), 64-column blocks — the coaxial line's numeric factorization 3.0 → 1.9 s (done); an extruded mesher for planar circuits was tried and dropped: the structured grid's long thin cells along strips' edges already suit their line singularity, and the extrusion took more tetrahedra; light subtrees of the supernodal tree on threads (the factor the same bit for bit), the ports' terms made once per frequency in a fast sweep — the coaxial line's 41 points: 144 s at the start, 79 s solving each in full, 14 s swept fast (done); the block's L used in place and W column-major, the panels' updates threaded on large fronts — 1.35 → 1.24 s; single precision tried and dropped (faer's complex f32 products are no faster here, so it would buy only memory); symmetry planes (done): `model.symmetry` puts an electric or magnetic wall on x = a/2 or y = b/2, only the half or quarter meshed, ports with the modes of the kept symmetry, fields beyond the plane by images — the iris band-pass filter's half matches the whole's mode matching (oracle `symmetry-half-filter`) with 54 912 of 108 974 unknowns, 1.0 s instead of 2.4 s; next: adaptive refinement, the time domain on the GPU | in progress |
| UI Desktop app | Phase 1 (done): SolidCraft-style shell — application bar, ribbon of the engine's commands in tabs (MODEL, MATERIALS, PORTS & SOURCES, MESH, SIMULATE, RESULTS) with original glyphs, command search, browser of the model and results, command dialogs generated from the parameter specs as floating or docked cards, messages; light and dark themes; long commands on a worker thread with progress and Cancel (`emcraft_engine::progress`); results views (model outline, S-parameters, fields with phase animation, port modes, eigenmodes, far field, comparison with theory); the control channel (`emcraft --control PORT`, docs/control-protocol.md) with a CPU `ui.render`; headless scenarios in `cargo test`. Phase 2 (workflow, docs/ux/workflow.md §5): the step bar 0 Intent … 8 Results with statuses from `project.status` and step panels (checks with links to fixes, the step's commands docked, the band, the port-mode preview, mesh statistics and the estimate, solver setup, Run's preflight refusing on blocking problems and accepting warnings, stale results, Touchstone export, passivity and reciprocity); a CPU 3D view (`emcraft_render::view3d`) of the mesh's boundary or the geometry's shapes with per-step overlays (materials, ports, walls and PML, mesh edges, and in Results the field's colour map on its plane inside the model), orbit, pan, zoom, fit, standard views, perspective/orthographic, orientation indicator; File (save, open, history as script) and Edit (undo, redo) menus. Fields on planes (done): text in pictures set in DejaVu Sans with anti-aliasing; the field view reads E and H under the pointer and at a pinned point (every component as amplitude and phase), its arrows follow the coloured field, H's between E's when both show, sliders with visible tracks; in the 3D view the plane carries its arrows and "Face the plane" frames it. SolidCraft-style start page and files (done): the start page (New project, Open, recent projects, sample cards with pictures, the units and look new projects start with), projects saved as `.emcraft` files with Save, Save As (the system's file dialog), Open recent, the title marking unsaved changes and the question before they are lost; the 3D view's tools as icons (a bar of fit, home, face the plane, projection, and a navigator of the six signed axes); each command once in the ribbon, view tabs only for views with something to show; TeX math in pictures' labels. Picking, planes and the GPU (done): a click in the 3D view selects a section, layer, strip, solid, feature, inclusion, port or field plane (ray picking), highlighted and tied to its browser row, a double-click edits it; several field planes kept for the 3D view on one colour scale; ports drawn before meshing (a coaxial line's annulus); the view drawn by the GPU through egui's painter while dragged or always (Settings ▸ 3D drawing ▸ Fast).
