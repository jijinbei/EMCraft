# Roadmap

EMCraft's goal is electromagnetic simulation on par with CST Studio Suite and HFSS for microwave
and RF components, open, verified and scriptable. Each milestone lands with oracle cases against
exact solutions or published benchmarks.

| Milestone | Scope | Status |
|---|---|---|
| M0 Foundation | Workspace, quality gates (layers, no-panic lints), SI model with display units, command engine with rollback and panic guard, scripts, CLI | done |
| M1 Waveguide high-pass filter | Structured tetrahedral meshes of rectangular waveguides in sections; Nédélec elements of orders 1 and 2 with exact element matrices; modal waveguide ports (TE and TM modes); multifrontal complex symmetric LDLᵀ with Bunch–Kaufman pivots, nested dissection, relaxed supernodes, blocked threaded fronts; power-normalised S-parameters; E and H fields; closed-form TE10 references; oracle cases (empty guide, dielectric step, high-pass filter) with fixtures; convergence-rate tests; field pictures (H-plane, E-plane, cross-section) and S-parameter charts as PNG; egui desktop app with phase animation | done |
| M2 Discontinuities | H-plane steps and inductive irises (sections with their own aperture across x, nesting from one to the next): meshes without the cells outside an aperture, graded geometrically towards re-entrant edges, port modes on offset apertures, power waves between ports of different aperture (done); mode matching with generalised scattering matrices as an independent reference (done); iris-coupled band-pass filter sample and oracle cases (done); E-plane steps and capacitive irises (apertures across y, LSE^x_1n mode matching, FEM and time domain against it) (done); posts, as Boolean features of an unstructured guide (M3) (done); partially overlapping apertures, steps in both width and height at once, comparison with published filter designs | in progress |
| M3 General geometry | The unstructured tetrahedral mesher ported from FieldCraft into `emcraft-tetmesh` (adaptive predicates, surface checks, Delaunay, constrained boundary recovery, refinement, smoothing, improvement; its 126 tests) (done); primitive bodies — box, cylinder, sphere — tessellated with exact surfaces and meshed, bucket point location, eigenmodes and driven solves on them, checked against Bessel-zero resonances and the TE10 guide (done); dielectric and conducting inclusions: each material region meshed against a frozen interface, the points boundary recovery adds passed between the sides until they agree, the regions joined node for node, checked against the two-layer spherical cavity (done); circular-waveguide ports with the TE/TM Bessel modes in both polarizations, checked against the TE11 phase (done); curved elements — the edges on curved walls and interfaces bent onto them, quadratic maps with the covariant Piola transform — taking the curved bodies' error from the O(h²) of flat facets (5.6e-3 at 3 mm for the cylinder) to 4e-5 (done); Boolean combinations of primitives with the body (`geometry.feature`: add, subtract, intersect; boxes, cylinders and spheres along any axis) by a mesh Boolean on exact predicates, flush faces moved past each other first, checked against mode matching on an iris made of two subtracted plates (done); imported surfaces — STL (binary, ASCII) and 3MF read by `emcraft-io`, grouped into faces by angle, ports on their end planes — checked against mode matching on a step read from STL (done); element sizes from the wavelength in each material (`per_wavelength`), each region of a solid at its own (done) | done |
| M4 Materials and losses | Lossy dielectrics (tan δ) in the model, solver and references, the discrete Poynting balance checked (done); finite-conductivity walls by the Leontovich surface-impedance condition, checked against the TE10 conductor attenuation and the wall-loss balance (done); Q factors (done with M5); ports in lossy media, dispersive materials, magnetic losses | in progress |
| M5 Eigenmodes | Cavity resonances by shift-invert subspace iteration (`solve.eigenmodes`, the gradient null space kept away by the shift), Q factors of walls and dielectrics by perturbation, mode pictures, `cavity` sample and oracle case (done); null-space deflation (tree–cotree) for modes far above the shift, lossy eigenproblems (complex frequencies), port mode solver for arbitrary cross-sections | in progress |
| M6 Time domain | Finite integration on tensor (non-uniform) hexahedral grids, threaded with deterministic results; TE10 pulse in PML-terminated feeds, broadband S-parameters by on-the-fly Fourier transforms against a uniform-guide reference run; checked against the exact cascade (second-order convergence) and mode matching (done); losses, multi-mode ports, conformal (sub-cell) edges, field monitors and animation | in progress |
| M7 Radiation | Absorbing boundaries and PML, far fields, antennas | planned |
